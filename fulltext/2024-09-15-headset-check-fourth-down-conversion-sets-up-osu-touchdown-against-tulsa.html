Headset check: Fourth-down conversion sets up OSU touchdown against Tulsa

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2024-09-15
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

No. 13 Oklahoma State handled business Saturday in its first road trip of the season against Tulsa, winning 45-10.

The win started off with a great opening drive that was capped off with a sure-handed touchdown grab by wideout Brennan Presley from his quarterback Alan Bowman.

That play would not have been possible without risky decision-making earlier in the drive. Instead of taking the easy three points inside of Tulsa's 10-yard line, head coach Mike Gundy and Co. decided to keep his eyes on the end zone.

On third-and-4, the Cowboys decided to hand the ball off and create a much more manageable fourth-and-1. On the 13th play of the drive, running back Ollie Gordon II was able to plow ahead to pick up the first down.

The Cowboys ability to come away with a touchdown instead of a field goal set the tone for the rest of the game, and they were able to hold onto that momentum.
