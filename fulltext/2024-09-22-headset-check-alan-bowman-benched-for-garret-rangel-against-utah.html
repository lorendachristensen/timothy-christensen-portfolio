Headset check: Alan Bowman benched for Garret Rangel against Utah

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2024-09-22
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

In the battle of the 20-year head coaches, Kyle Whittingham and No. 12 Utah defeated Mike Gundy and No. 14 Oklahoma State 22-19.

The Cowboy offense struggled to get the ball moving in its Big 12 opener. Despite putting up the first points of the game, the unit did not contribute much in the first half, as OSU trailed 10-3 at halftime

The offensive struggles brought on a quarterback change, as seventh-year quarterback Alan Bowman was benched for redshirt sophomore Garret Rangel.

But the OSU (3-1, 0-1 Big 12) offense continued to struggle with Rangel, as he finished his outing 3-of-11 for 31 yards. The miscues caused the home crowd to chant, “Bring back Bowman!" Eventually, Bowman was put back in the game, and despite the late game rally from the offense, the Cowboys weren't able to complete the comeback against Utah (4-0, 1-0 Big 12).

When asked about the quarterback change after the game, OSU head coach Mike Gundy used the term “relieve pitcher” to explain benching Bowman.

"Sometimes you got to get a guy out to calm him down a little bit," Gundy said.

The quarterback struggles were not what the Cowboys were hoping for in this important Big 12 opener, and they'll hope to bounce back next week with another big game in Manhattan, Kansas, against No. 13 Kansas State.
