Headset check: OSU opting for the conservative route against Kansas State

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2024-09-30
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

No. 20 Oklahoma State lost a second straight game in a pivotal Big 12 matchup against No. 23 Kanas State 42-20.

K-State (4-1, 1-1 Big 12) started the game strong, marching down the field on its first possession to grab the first touchdown of the game. During the opening drive, the Wildcats and head coach Chris Klieman decided to go for two fourth-down conversions and were successful in both thanks to passes from quarterback Avery Johnson.

In comparison, OSU (3-2, 0-2 Big 12) and head coach Mike Gundy decided on the very next drive to take the three points instead of risking a fourth-and-2 attempt.

The play before the fourth down decision consisted of a handoff up the middle to running back Ollie Gordon II for two yards, and the offense was almost immediately taken off the field in favor of the field goal unit. It seemed that the coaching staff had already made up its mind on the plan if the first down was not picked up.

Hindsight is always 20/20, but the risky play calling from the K-State paid off with seven points and gave them early momentum, while OSU's conservative decision making early in the game put field goals on the board for a team that would later desperately need more touchdowns.
