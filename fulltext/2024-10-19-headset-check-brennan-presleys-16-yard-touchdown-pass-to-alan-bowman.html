Headset check: Brennan Presley's 16-yard touchdown pass to Alan Bowman

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2024-10-19
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State lost 38-35 in heartbreaking fashion Friday night on the road against No. 13 BYU.

OSU (3-4, 0-4 Big 12) had the lead with 19 seconds left in the game, but the Cougars (7-0, 4-0 Big 12) were able to secure the win with a late 35-yard touchdown pass from quarterback Jake Retzlaff to wideout Darius Lassiter.

As pressure from fans has been building for offensive coordinator Kasey Dunn, he and the rest of the offensive coaching staff faced lots of adversity against BYU. The Cowboys lost starting quarterback Garret Rangel to injury near the end of the first half and returned seventh-year Alan Bowman to assume the position.

The Cowboys had a total of 71 yards passing entering the fourth quarter, and Dunn knew he had to get something dialed up for the offense. On the first play of the quarter, Bowman threw a lateral to star wideout Brennan Presley, just for Presley to throw it right back to Bowman with blockers in front of him. The play ended with a 16-yard touchdown, marking Bowman’s first career receiving touchdown and Presley’s first career passing touchdown.

Play-calling like this helped the Cowboy offense score 35 points even with its quarterback struggles. Although it was not enough this week, they hope that they continue to improve when they head to Waco next week to take on the Baylor.
