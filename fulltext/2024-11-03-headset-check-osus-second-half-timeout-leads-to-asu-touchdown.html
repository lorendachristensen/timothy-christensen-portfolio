Headset check: OSU's second-half timeout leads to ASU touchdown

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2024-11-03
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State had a long day Saturday, both literally and figuratively, as the Cowboys lost their sixth straight game to Arizona State, 42-21.

Even with a two-hour-and-38-minute weather delay, OSU (3-6, 0-6 Big 12) were never able to get things going. But in reality, the Cowboys' struggles started before the delay.

On ASU's (6-2, 3-2 Big 12) final drive of the first half, it had made it to OSU’s 32-yard line. ASU decided to run the ball on first-and-20 and gained 8 yards from running back Cam Skattebo.

Head coach Mike Gundy then immediately called a timeout after that play to stop the clock at 1:47. Now, the reasoning for making this decision is sound — Gundy wanted his offense to get the ball back with plenty of time remaining on the clock.

That goal did not end up coming to fruition. It is easy to critique with the benefit of hindsight, but with how the Cowboy defense has been performing this season and the Sun Devils’ kicking struggles, ASU deciding if it should chew the clock and try for a field goal was a real option.

Whether Gundy’s decision was correct or not, the end result did not go his way. ASU scored a touchdown on the drive and left OSU with no timeouts and 20 seconds in the half.

The last two minutes of the first half probably left a bad taste in everyone’s mouth during the delayed halftime.
