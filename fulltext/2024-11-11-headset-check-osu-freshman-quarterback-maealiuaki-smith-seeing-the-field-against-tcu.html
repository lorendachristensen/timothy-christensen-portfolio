Headset check: OSU freshman quarterback Maealiuaki Smith seeing the field against TCU

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2024-11-11
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State officially lost its bowl eligibility for the first time in 18 years this past Saturday after a 38-13 loss to TCU.

The nighttime matchup did not go well for the Cowboys (3-7, 0-7 Big 12) in a situation that has become familiar territory for this team — falling behind early and then making changes late in the game.

One of the changes head coach Mike Gundy made late in the game is benching quarterback Alan Bowman for a second straight week for freshman quarterback Maealiuaki Smith. Despite an ugly-looking interception on a fourth down, Smith finished 6-of-8 passing for 92 yards.

Smith’s play in his limited opportunities this year leads to the question of whether Gundy should have made the move earlier or even just go into the game with Smith as the starter. The first reason to start him is to be able to see what you have in the young player, getting to play along star players like Ollie Gordon II and now OSU’s all-time receptions leader Brennan Presley.

The other reason to start Smith is Bowman has struggled greatly in the past seven games, and OSU has not won any of them. Depending on what Smith is showing at practice, he might now give the Cowboys the best chance to win. With a bowl game out of the picture and two games remaining, the future may be the best thing to focus on.
