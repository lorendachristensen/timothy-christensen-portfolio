Headset check: Ollie Gordon II pass puts OSU in position to find the end zone

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2024-11-25
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State remained winless in Big 12 Conference play after Saturday’s Senior Day game, losing 56-48 to Texas Tech.

The game was full of points, so to remain competitive in the shootout, the Cowboy coaching staff attempted to pull out a trick.

It was a wildcat play with running back Ollie Gordon II taking the snap. With the help of a flea flicker, Gordon was able to complete a pass to wide receiver Rashod Owens for a 27-yard gain.

Four plays later, Gordon had a 1-yard rushing touchdown, making his first completion at OSU even more impactful.

However, even though it tied the score up 14-14 in the second quarter, OSU didn’t have enough in the long run to come out on top.
