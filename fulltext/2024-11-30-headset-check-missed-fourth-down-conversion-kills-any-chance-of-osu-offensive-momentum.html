Headset check: Missed fourth-down conversion kills any chance of OSU offensive momentum

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2024-11-30
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

The 2024 season ended sourly Friday for Oklahoma State, which lost to Colorado 52-0.

The game was never particularly close for head coach Mike Gundy and the Cowboys, as mistakes and penalties became a familiar sight throughout the contest.

In the middle of the third quarter, it seemed like OSU’s offense was finding some rhythm down 35-0. But after a couple of short passes to wide receiver Brennan Presley and an Ollie Gordon II run for no gain, the Cowboys found themselves at a fourth-and-1. Trying to find any momentum, Gundy decided to go for the first down.

Offensive coordinator Kasey Dunn called a pass to Brennan Presley on a short drag route. Quarterback Maealiuaki Smith’s pass fell incomplete, giving the ball back to the Buffaloes.

Despite the blowout, that play was the only fourth-down attempt for the Cowboys.
