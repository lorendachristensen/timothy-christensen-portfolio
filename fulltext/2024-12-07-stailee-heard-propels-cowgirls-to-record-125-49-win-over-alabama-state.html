Stailee Heard propels Cowgirls to record 125-49 win over Alabama State

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2024-12-07
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State had another record-breaking performance in its 76-point win Friday night against the Alabama State in Gallagher Iba Arena.

The Cowgirls set the OSU record for most points scored in a game in program history with 125 points. Holding the Hornets to 49 points, OSU (9-1) marked yet another school record in point differential.

Sophomore standout Stailee Heard’s 3-point shooting helped her reach a career-high 32 points. On the night, she made eight of 11 3s, contributing to 61% from the field. Heard was able to help in other ways, totaling 10 rebounds and tying her career assist record with six.

The hot streak for Heard started OSU's previous game against Houston Christian, where she went the perfect seven for seven from 3. Add that up with her start Friday, and she was able to make 12 straight 3s through six quarters of play. Heard’s feat helped the team shoot 52% from 3 on the night.

“(OSU head coach Jacie Hoyt’s) been telling me to shoot the ball all season long,” Heard said. “I just have to have the self-confidence (that) I can shoot the ball… so just got to let it fly.”

In the first eight games of the season, Heard had 19 3-point attempts. In the last two games, she has attempted, leading to records and success.

“She and I — we both share this drive to be the best we can, and sometimes, as a player, maybe you don't even realize some things,” Hoyt said. “That's the job of a coach, to bring that out in (players), and I'm so thankful I get to do that with (Heard) because we've had a lot of fun doing it. “

And Hoyt is having fun watching Heard prove the doubters wrong.

“A lot of people didn't recruit (Heard) because they said she couldn't shoot,” Hoyt said. “I'm so glad that they didn't because it's fun having her as a Cowgirl.”
