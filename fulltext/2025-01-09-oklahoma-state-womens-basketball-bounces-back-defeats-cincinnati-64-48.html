Oklahoma State women's basketball bounces back, defeats Cincinnati 64-48

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2025-01-09
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Despite an ugly first half performance, Oklahoma State was able to bounce back from last week’s loss against Kansas by defeating Cincinnati 64-48.

The Wednesday night performance was pivotal for the Cowgirls. After a losing to Kansas, the Cowgirls needed to get their season back on track to remain firmly in the Big 12 race, and they did what they needed to do to accomplish that goal.

However, the victory was not particularly pretty in the shooting department.

OSU’s poor shooting from the KU game followed them into the Bearcat matchup as well, starting the night making zero of its first nine shots from the 3-point arc. It took until 5:38 in the second quarter for the Cowgirls to hit their first from beyond the arc, courtesy of sophomore standout Stailee Heard.

As the game continued, the Cowgirls were able to find their footing. OSU shot four-of-ten in the second half from 3-point range, which was much more in line with their season average 3-point percentage.

Despite the inconsistent shooting, there were lots of positives to come out of the game for OSU. The Cowgirls did not give up many easy shots to Cincinnati’s offense, holding them to a 30% field goal shooting percentage. The win was also aided by strength at the rim, collecting 49 rebounds, 13 of which came from Heard.

Despite some struggles, head coach Jacie Hoyt was pleased with how the game turned out.

“I knew it was going to be a game like that, because Cincinnati is just tough,” Hoyt stated. “They certainly made it hard on us but love the fourth quarter. Love the second half response. We just found a way. And sometimes you just got to find a way.”

Overall, the night was a success for the Hoyt and the Cowgirls. The win marked their third in conference play and showed that they are not overly reliant on the 3-point shot.

Hoyt was excited about the night and is excited about the to Gallagher-Iba Arena on Saturday.

“The focus was bouncing back,” Hoyt said. “Obviously, we were not happy with the results of that Kansas game, but it's all about how you respond. And I love our response tonight. Now we got another big game back home.”
