Stailee Heard continues dominant season in OSU's 83-71 win against Arizona State

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2025-01-30
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State extended its Big 12 winning streak to four by defeating Arizona State 83-71 in Gallagher-Iba Arena.

The No. 24 Cowgirls spent the majority of the matchup with a comfortable lead. Despite being down 6-8 early in the first quarter, OSU pulled off a 16-2 run to close out the quarter. The Cowgirls never trailed again in the contest.

A three-pointer from sophomore standout Stailee Heard started the impressive end-of-quarter run for the Cowgirls. Heard finished the night with five made 3’s and a team-leading nine rebounds and 28 points.

Heard’s night comes off a career-high 34 points the game prior against Texas Tech. She's now played all 80 minutes of the games against TTU and ASU, helping the Cowgirls craft four straight conference wins.

“(Heard is) a matchup nightmare,” Adair stated. “She can post up the littles. She can shoot it from the perimeter. She plays hard as I don't know what”

Heard wasn't the only Cowgirl to have a good game. Senior Anna Gret Asi was able to tally up 14 points and four assists, while fifth-year Ténin Magassa provided 11 points to go along with some stingy defense to collect 3 blocks.

The win will help keep the Cowgirls in the rankings, which the Cowgirls were not able to do the last time they were ranked No. 24 this season.

Head coach Jacie Hoyt explained why this team has made so much progress from last year.

“Just a sign of getting the right kids,” Hoyt said. “We certainly have that, and that's why this season has been so fun. Take the winning out. It's about process, and we're just loving each other process, and that's really what it's about.”

OSU will hope to continue perfecting the process as they continue through their Big 12 schedule, with top-25 matchups against West Virginia and Kansas State next up.
