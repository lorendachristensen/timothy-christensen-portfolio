Oklahoma State equestrian wins fifth straight Big 12 title

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2025-03-29
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State equestrian earned its fifth consecutive Big 12 title Saturday afternoon.

In a matchup against No. 1 TCU at Bear Creek Farms in Fort Worth, Texas, the 11-8 victory extends the Cowgirls' record for most Big 12 titles in a row after breaking the record last year. It also brings OSU’s overall Big 12 trophy count to 11.

The Horned Frogs defeated No. 4 Fresno State 15-4 to make it to the finals. No. 2 OSU had a tougher round as it survived No. 3 Baylor 11-9 to join the Horned Frogs in the final meet.

The matchup was probably a lot of déjà vu for the Cowgirls after they competed against the Horned Frogs in the finals last season. In both cases, OSU went into the meet having gone 0-2 against TCU in the regular season.

The Cowgirls had a strong first half and led 7-3 going into halftime. The Horned Frogs answered with a 4-1 run in the Flat portion, bringing OSU’s lead down to one.

The team's loss of a cushion in their lead didn’t bring it down, however. With three of the five riders winning in the Reigning portion and one tie, OSU was once again able to bring a Big 12 Championship back to Stillwater.

The Cowgirls will head to Ocala, Florida, next to compete for the national title. The tournament starts April 17.
