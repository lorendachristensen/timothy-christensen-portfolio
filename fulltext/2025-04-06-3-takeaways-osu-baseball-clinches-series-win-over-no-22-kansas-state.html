3 Takeaways: OSU baseball clinches series win over No.22 Kansas State

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2025-04-06
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State won its second game of the series against the No. 22 Kansas State Wildcats 7-0. The game was the first of the Sunday afternoon doubleheader for the Cowboys.

Here are three takeaways from the matchup:

Youngerman impresses on the mound

The victory was aided by the impressive performance from pitcher Sean Youngerman, who ended the game with 11 strikeouts and only allowed three hits on the day. Youngerman's velocity and power on the mound were on display throughout his eight innings.

Youngerman pitched a perfect first five innings before surrendering a single to CF Donte Lewis at the top of the sixth inning. Youngerman only allowed four players on base the entire day.

The 11 strikeouts are a career-high for Youngerman in division one baseball, smashing his former record of five against Texas State earlier this season. Youngerman also had an 11-strikeout game in his division two days at Westmont College.

The pitching performance helped the OSU complete the shutout, their third of the year.

Three different Cowboys hit home runs

The Cowboys have struggled this season when it comes to getting on base and earning runs. Even in the win in the first game of the series, OSU only scored four times and relied on Harrison Bodendorf to have an impressive pitching day. Sunday showed the best of both worlds, combining the production on the mound with some home runs to go along with it.

Right fielder Nolan Schubart put up the first point of the game in the bottom of the first inning when he hammered the ball into right field. His run remained the only of the game until the bottom of the fourth inning.

When left fielder Kollin Ritchie came up to bat, it didn’t take long for him to get swinging. On the first pitch of his at-bat, Ritchie was able to earn the Cowboys another home run, this time garnering three runs.

In the sixth inning, catcher Beau Sylvester got a home run of his own in what ended up being the final score of the game.

Brayden Smith continues his hitting streak

Second baseman Brayden Smith came up to bat in the fourth inning with runners on first and second base. Smith already had a strikeout and a ground out and needed to make something happen. With the count being 2-2, Smith did just that.

Smith’s hit bounced right between K-State's center and right fielders and made its way over to the wall. The hit resulted in a triple and provided OSU with two more runs.

The hit was important to the Cowboys as it extended their lead, but the hit also helped Smith continue his impressive hitting streak to a whopping 14 games. The hitting streak has greatly helped his .301-hitting average on the season. During the streak, he has hit two triples, five doubles, and five homers.

The win captured the series for the Cowboys, who needed to get some wins on the board in the Big 12.
