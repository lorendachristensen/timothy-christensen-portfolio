Hoyt adds height, depth in Winlie Joseph through transfer portal

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2025-05-15
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State head coach Jacie Hoyt has, once again, drawn in another recruit from the transfer portal with a 6-foot-5 center from McNeese State, Wilnie Joseph.

OSU marks the fourth team Joseph has joined during her collegiate career as she enters her senior year.

Joseph spent two seasons at Labette Community College in Parsons, KS, where she appeared in a total of 42 games. She then transferred to the University of Louisiana at Lafayette, where she appeared in 19 games and averaged 1.4 points and 1.7 rebounds in one season.

Last year, Joseph played with McNeese State, where she started 30 out of 31 games, averaging 3.8 points and 4.9 rebounds and tallying season totals of 13 blocks and 14 steals. When McNeese State faced off against OSU in December this past season, Joseph played 24 minutes, scored four points and collected five rebounds.

Joseph marks the seventh player Hoyt and the Cowgirls have added in the portal as they attempt to replace graduates from the program. The 6-foot-6 Tenin Magassa, in particular, showed the value that having a big on this team can provide.

Hoyt seems to be putting a big emphasis on having reliable players who can collect rebounds and guard the paint. Last year, the team was heavily dependent on Magassa for that role because she was the only player on the squad over 6-foot-3, creating a hefty expectation for Joseph out of the gate.

The hope for OSU is that Joseph can provide more depth at the center position as she joins fellow transfer Favour Onoh from the Philippines. With both standing at a towering 6-foot-5, these portal moves by Hoyt and Co. add height to both floor and the bench players.

Hoyt has proven with players such as Tenin Magassa and Alexia Smith that she is capable of taking those who are in the twilight of their collegiate careers and developing them to another level. Perhaps Joseph will be another example of that.
