Kickoff times set for OSU football’s first three games

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2025-05-29
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State football fans can now start planning their game day schedules with the times of OSU’s first three matchups against UT Martin, Oregon and Tulsa being announced Thursday morning.

The Cowboys started their 2024 campaign with a 3-0 record against South Dakota State, Arkansas and Tulsa. That may be the only stretch coach Mike Gundy would want to remember from a disappointing season.

The first game of the season will be in Boone Pickens Stadium against UT Martin at 6:30 p.m. CT on August 28. If not attending in person, OSU fans will be able to watch it on ESPN+.

This game is the only one scheduled on a Thursday this season for the Cowboys. The UT Martin matchup continues OSU’s trend of starting the year against a lower-tier opponent, with season openers from the last 3 years being Central Arkansas, Central Michigan and South Dakota State.

On September 6th at 2:30 p.m. CT, OSU will seek to shock the college football world in Eugene, taking on Dan Lanning and the Oregon Ducks. The matchup will air on CBS Sports.

Beating the Ducks early in the season will be an uphill battle for the Cowboys. Oregon spent the majority of last season as the undefeated No. 1 team in both the AP Top 25 and the College Football Playoff Top 25.

After losing their first game of the playoffs, there is no doubt that the Ducks want to start 2025 out on the right foot. While Oregon has had a lot of roster turnover this offseason, the Cowboys have had even more and are creating new systems with two new coordinators. Stealing a victory on the road is certainly not going to be easy.

OSU will have a short week when it comes back to Stillwater to take on the Tulsa Golden Hurricanes on Friday, September 19th at 6 p.m. CT on ESPN.

The matchup continues the now-annual Gateway First Bank Turnpike Classic series for the in-state rivals. Oklahoma State holds the all-time record in the series 44-27-5. When the two teams faced off last season, OSU won convincingly in Tulsa, 45-10. Now, the Golden Hurricanes will try to avenge that loss in Boone Pickens Stadium.

The two home openers for the Cowboys will both be night games, which is already an improvement from last year as they received zero night games the entire season in BPS.

Minus the road trip against Oregon, the non-conference games will certainly be winnable for the Cowboys. Even if they start the season 2-1, it could be extremely beneficial for a team full of new players to find some continuity and leave a wrecked 2024 season behind them before taking on the Big 12.
