OSU shut out of Big 12 preseason honors

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2025-07-07
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Just a day before Big 12 Media Days, the conference released its All-Big 12 offensive and defensive teams. The media members who vote on the teams are tasked with evaluating who is expected to be the best players at each position in the upcoming season.

Oklahoma State players were nowhere to be found on the list. OSU joins Arizona, Houston and UCF as the only four Big 12 teams without a player on the 2025 team.

The defending Big 12 champions, Arizona State, had a conference-high five players to make the team, and returning quarterback Sam Leavitt was chosen as the Preseason Offensive Player of the Year. Iowa State and Texas Tech tied for second place with three players, as the Red Raiders’ Jacob Rodriguez was named the Preseason Defensive Player of the Year.

This year’s results for OSU are a far cry from the 2024, when Mike Gundy’s crew sported a conference-leading six players on the preseason team, including Ollie Gordon II being named the 2024 Preseason Offensive Player of the Year. The roster had a league-leading 22 returning starters who were anticipated to bring continuity and experience with them into the 2024 season.

Just a year later, the situation has shifted. After going 3-9 and winless in the conference last season, OSU has moved on from both of its coordinators and has lost all six of the players to make the 2024 Preseason All-Big 12 team to the NFL.

Other than retaining head coach Mike Gundy for his 21st season, continuity is not an adjective that will be attributed to this 2025 OSU football team.

There are some highly anticipated questions awaiting Gundy heading into the Big 12 Media Days to address the influx of changes in his program, and it wouldn’t be a surprise if discussion on the lack of names on that preseason list made an appearance.
