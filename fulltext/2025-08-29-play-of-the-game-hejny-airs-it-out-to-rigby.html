Play of the Game: Hejny airs it out to Rigby

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2025-08-29
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

After a long offseason, Oklahoma State football fans once again had something to cheer for in Boone Pickens Stadium as OSU defeated UT Martin 27-7.

Up 7-0 with 7 minutes left in the first quarter, Hejny and the offense lined up wanting to add onto their lead. After receiving the snap, Hejny immediately rolled out to his right.

With pressure in his face, Hejny launched the ball to wide receiver Shamar Rigby, who was able to turn around and make the grab. The play resulted in a 41-yard gain and a first down, contributing to the touchdown-scoring drive.

The early 14-point lead gave the Cowboys a nice cushion, especially for an offense that had some moments of struggle. Despite leaving the game with a leg injury, Hejny made his presence on the game felt, and time will tell when he will be able to get back on the football field.
