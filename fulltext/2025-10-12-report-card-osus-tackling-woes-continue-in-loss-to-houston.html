Report card: OSU's tackling woes continue in loss to Houston

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2025-10-12
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State once again lost to a Big 12 opponent, this time Houston, after coming into the weekend with multiple quarterback injuries.

Here’s a report card from Saturday's game.

The wide receiver Sam Jackson V got his chance to play the highly acclaimed quarterback position, and the results were… mixed.

Jackson V was part of a struggling offense all game, with the only touchdowns scored being on a trick play and a drive following his benching in the fourth quarter.

While the offensive struggles were certainly not all on the quarterback, Jackson V’s passing and rushing stats were below what was needed

Houston quarterback Conner Weigman bounced back from a tough performance last week against Texas Tech, passing for 306 yards and 2 touchdowns.

Weigman was able to take advantage of gaps in OSU’s passing defense while making some impressive throws in the middle of the field.

Weigman was able to statistically have his best performance of an already very impressive year for the Cougars.

While Weigman made some impressive throws, the OSU defense also provided him with lots of passing yards after the catch.

The tackling wasn’t just a problem in the passing game. Houston running back Dean Connors had a great day, rarely being taken down on first contact.

Tackling has been an issue for the Cowboys all season, and it hasn’t shown signs of improvement so far.
