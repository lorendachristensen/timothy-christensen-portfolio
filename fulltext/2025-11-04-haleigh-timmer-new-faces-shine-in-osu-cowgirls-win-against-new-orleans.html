Haleigh Timmer, new faces shine in OSU Cowgirls win against New Orleans

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2025-11-04
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State was able to start the women’s basketball season strong Monday night when it took on the New Orleans Pioneers, winning 109-48.

Despite picking up right where they left off last year’s historic season, the game showcased the Cowgirls' new faces for fans in attendance at Gallagher-Iba Arena.

Coach Jacie Hoyt and OSU worked in the offseason to load up on experienced talent from the transfer portal. They brought in six players with Division I experience, with a total of 388 games played and 2,937 points split between them all.

One of the new Cowgirls who already made an impact was South Dakota State transfer Haleigh Timmer.

Timmer played a large role in creating the Cowgirls' lead, putting up a team-high 16 points in 15 minutes during the first half. Timmer ended the night with 18 points and six assists and shot 4 of 8 from beyond the 3-point line.

Some players might have trouble fitting in with a new team during their senior year, but Timmer does not see that as an issue.

“I picked Oklahoma State because it felt like home when I visited here,” Timmer said. “I think that made the acclimation simple and easy. It just speaks to the coaching staff and community… I really didn’t struggle to settle in at all.”

Hoyt feels that the Cowgirls are poised to reap the benefits of the addition of Timmer.

“We lost a lot [of players] last year,” Hoyt said. “I feel like she was the perfect person to come in and really help fill some big holes. We had a great season last year, but we want to take a step forward, and I think that she's the perfect person to help us do that, because she knows what it takes.”

The junior transfer from BYU, Amari Whiting, was also able to fill the stat sheet, racking up 11 points, eight rebounds, five assists and three steals. Whiting flew around the court for the majority of her playing time.

Other new faces who made their Cowgirl debuts were Lina Girardi and Faith Acker, who put up 15 and eight points, respectively.
