Cowboys take 2-1 series lead against GCU with 10-7 win

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-02-22
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State was able to take a 2-1 series lead over Grand Canyon Saturday evening with a 10-7 win.

With the combination of poor pitching and exciting batting, the matchup provided lots of runs for both sides. The Cowboys found their offensive explosion in the third and fourth innings, collecting nine of their 10 runs.

At the top of the third, junior Kollin Ritchie was able to put up two RBIs with a triple down the right-field foul line. Aidan Meola’s ball also found its way to right field, except it made it out of the park for a three-run home run.

After GCU made a pitching change to fifth-year senior Jacob Limas, Cowboy Terrance Owen hit a single into left field to get Sebastian Norman home.

Limas’ day didn’t fare much better in the fourth inning, surrendering four hits, two RBIs, one walk and a wild pitch that the Cowboys were able to capitalize on to get Meola home once again.

OSU's offensive fortune came to a screeching halt, however, when GCU brought in senior Jace Smith in relief. Smith was able to retire six straight batters in his stints in the fourth and fifth innings, and ended his day with five strikeouts, zero walks, two hits, and one run.

Relief pitchers Dilon Orr and Cam Cunnings also did their job, allowing two and one hits respectively, and not allowing the Cowboys to score again after the sixth inning.

Coach Josh Holliday and OSU’s batting strategy was clear: swing early and often.

There was not a lot of waiting for the perfect pitches; the goal was to make contact with the ball, and either get a good hit or foul ball to keep the at-bat alive.

The OSU pitching lineup was inconsistent throughout the day, but it was good enough to get the job done.

Starting pitcher Mario Pesca had a rollercoaster of a day in his three innings, giving up two runs in the first inning, zero in the second and three in the third. Pesca ended his day with three strikeouts, three walks, three hits and five runs.

Relief pitcher Kai Fyke was a bright spot for the Cowboys, allowing only a single run in three innings. Despite not having a strikeout, Fyke also did not give away a walk, making GCU earn runners on base.

Sophomore Noah Wech was able to close out the game for the Cowboys, allowing only one hit and collecting two strikeouts in the final two innings.

The win guarantees OSU at least a tie in the series, and they will finish it out Sunday evening in Phoenix.
