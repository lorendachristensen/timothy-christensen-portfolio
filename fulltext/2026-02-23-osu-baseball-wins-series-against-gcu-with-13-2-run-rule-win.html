OSU baseball wins series against GCU with 13-2 run-rule win

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-02-23
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State finished its Grand Canyon series in dominant fashion on Sunday with a run-rule score of 13-2 in seven innings, recovering from the tough Game 1 loss to win the series 3-1.

While the high scoring was par for the course, another development for the Cowboys was the five innings they got from sophomore pitcher Ethan Lund.

Lund only pitched nine total innings all of last season for the Cowboys, but has already pitched eight in the early parts of this year’s campaign.

On Sunday, Lund finished with six strikeouts and allowed five walks, two hits and two runs. While certainly not the perfect game, it provided a steady arm for the majority of the day and limited the number of pitchers used at the end of this weekend’s series.

In a postgame radio interview, OSU coach Josh Holliday was quick to temper praise and leave room for improvement for the young player.

“He pitched fine,” Holliday said. “He pitched good. But there's a high standard when you know what that could look like. I didn't understand visually why the lefties matched his stuff. So that part I'm anxious to see him solve, because I think what he does… this kid's pretty special.”

On the offensive end, it was a familiar sight throughout the weekend for the Cowboys, putting lots of runs on the scoreboard.

This time, the scoring started early.

Although OSU had suffered two early outs, Aidan Meola was able to hit a single into right field that allowed teammate Brock Thompson to score from first base. After gaining another baserunner, Colin Brueggemann sent the ball over the center-field wall for the three-run homer.

After stealing his way to third base with the help of a GCU throwing error, Terrance Bowen was able to score the fifth and final run of the impressive inning, thanks to a single by Alex Conover.

OSU’s next score didn’t come until the fourth inning, where Aidan Meola was able to take advantage of his 2-1 count and hit the ball out of left field for a grand slam, breaking the game wide open for the Cowboys.

Kollin Ritchie was able to put the finishing touches on the game in the seventh inning, adding a three-run homer in left field.

This series provided some added clarity on the strengths and weaknesses of the team. Holliday and the squad will certainly find things to work on heading into a nine-game homestand.

“I'm encouraged,” Holliday said. “Three out of four is acceptable. It's not what we set out to do, but it’s what we did. So now we take this momentum and what we learned, and we get on the plane and get home. Man, can't wait to see all the awesome cowboy fans on Tuesday.”
