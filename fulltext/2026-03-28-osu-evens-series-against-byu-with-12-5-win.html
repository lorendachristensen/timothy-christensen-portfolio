OSU evens series against BYU with 12-5 win

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-03-28
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State was able to bounce back from its Game 1 loss in a big way, beating BYU 12-5 to even up the series on Friday.

A lot went right for the Cowboys on the offensive and defensive ends, a marked improvement from the walk-off loss suffered the night prior.

Despite a couple of missteps from the senior, Mario Pesca was able to provide a steady hand for OSU’s pitching.

Pesca was able to find the strike zone numerous times during his six and a half innings, striking out seven batters and retiring another 13, while also surrendering six hits, three runs and two walks.

Junior Kai Fyke finished out the final two and a half innings on the mound for OSU with two hits, runs and strikeouts.

While not the perfect outings for the pitching staff, they were enough to keep BYU’s scoring minimal and let the offense feel comfortable for most of the night. The Cowboys have also managed to utilize only four total pitchers in the first two games of this series, likely saving some bodies for the final game on Saturday.

The bats were certainly swinging for the Cowboys, garnering 12 runs and two home runs.

The first of the home runs came in the top of the fifth from Garret Shull, hitting past the left field as the ball rolled onto Campus Drive next to the Miller Park Complex. Shull’s hit brought him and Danny Wallace home.

The second of the home runs came from the Cowboys' home run leader, Kollin Ritchie, launching the ball into left field for his 15th home run of the season, driving in two runs for the Cowboys.

To add on to the impressive performance from the Cowboys’ batting lineup, OSU was also gifted two runs on wild pitches from the BYU mound.

Josh Holliday and the rest of the team will certainly want to keep their offensive momentum heading into the series finale on Saturday.
