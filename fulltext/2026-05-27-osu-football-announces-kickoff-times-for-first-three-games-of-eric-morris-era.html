OSU football announces kickoff times for first three games of Eric Morris era

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-05-27
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State football and its fans can start planning their gameday schedules with the announcement of kickoff times for the first three games against Tulsa, Oregon and Murray State, as well as the TV network for their first Big 12 opponent, West Virginia.

These games mark not only the beginning of the 2026 campaign, but also the start of new head coach Eric Morris' tenure at OSU. The offseason has brought renewed optimism around the Cowboys, coming off a 1-11 season.

Morris and his staff have been active in the transfer portal, bringing in several former North Texas players, including quarterback Drew Mestemaker, running back Caleb Hawkins and wide receiver Wyatt Young.

The coaching change and offseason additions have brought renewed energy to the fanbase, and OSU hopes to carry that momentum into the start of the season.

The first game of the season will take place an hour east of Stillwater, as the Cowboys travel to face the Tulsa Golden Hurricane at 2:45 p.m. CT on Sept. 5. Fans unwilling to make the trip can watch the game on ESPNU.

The matchup carries added significance for OSU after last season's 19-12 home loss to Tulsa, a defeat that marked the end of Mike Gundy's tenure as head coach. Morris and the Cowboys will look to reverse that result this season.

The home opener in Boone Pickens Stadium against the Oregon Ducks will take place Sept. 12 at 11 a.m. CT. The game will also air on ESPN.

Last season's matchup in Eugene was one-sided, as the Cowboys lost 69-3. The 66-point margin was OSU's largest defeat since 1907. Oregon later earned the No. 5 seed in the College Football Playoff and lost to eventual national champion Indiana in the Peach Bowl, 56-22. Dan Lanning and the Ducks enter the season looking to build on last year's playoff appearance.

The offseason moves and home-field advantage should make the game more competitive than OSU's last outing, but pulling out the win will still be an uphill climb.

The latest kickoff time of the 2026 season will come against the Murray State Racers on Sept. 19 at 6 p.m. CT, with the game available on ESPN+.

This continues OSU's recent trend of scheduling FCS opponents early in the season, including last year's matchup with UT Martin, which produced the Cowboys' lone win of the year. The game could provide an opportunity for OSU to regroup following its matchup with Oregon.

While a kickoff time for this game has not yet been announced, it will air on FS1. The matchup in Morgantown will mark the start of OSU's Big 12 schedule as the Cowboys seek their first conference victory since 2023.
