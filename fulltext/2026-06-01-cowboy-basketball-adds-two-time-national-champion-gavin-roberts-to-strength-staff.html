Cowboy basketball adds two-time national champion Gavin Roberts to strength staff

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-06-01
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State announced Monday that Gavin Roberts will join Steve Lutz's staff as the program's new strength and conditioning coach.

Roberts comes from UConn, where he served as director of sports performance and was part of the Huskies' back-to-back national championship teams in 2023 and 2024. Since 2021, Roberts has overseen all aspects of strength and conditioning, nutrition and wellness for the program.

Before his stint at UConn, Roberts spent three years as a strength coach at Purdue, where he worked alongside Lutz during Lutz's four-year tenure with the Boilermakers. Roberts also earned his degree from Purdue while playing defensive back for the football team.

"Gavin has been part of championship-level programs and understands what it takes to prepare players to perform at their best," Lutz said in a release. "I saw that firsthand when we worked together at Purdue. He brings energy, expertise and a very intentional approach to developing athletes, and he will be a tremendous resource for our players."

Roberts' arrival adds another familiar face to Lutz's staff as the Cowboys continue building toward the 2026-27 season.

"I'm excited to be at Oklahoma State and to work alongside Coach Lutz and this staff," Roberts said. "This program has great tradition, and I'm ready to help our players build the daily standard that it takes to win."
