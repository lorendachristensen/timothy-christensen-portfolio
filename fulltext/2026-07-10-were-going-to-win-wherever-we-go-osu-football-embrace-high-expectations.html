'We're going to win wherever we go': OSU football embrace high expectations

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-07-10
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

FRISCO, Texas — The Big 12 held the first half of its annual Media Days on Tuesday, during which the media spoke with Oklahoma State football players, including quarterback Drew Mestemaker, running back Caleb Hawkins, linebacker Ethan Wesloski and defensive end Jaleel Johnson.

Here are some notable statements from the players:

"For me, I've been able to understand schematics," Wesloski said. "I am in my fourth year starting, but it'll be my fifth year in college football. I've been around a lot of football; I understand what offenses like to go to in distances and situations."

Wesloski spoke on how he feels that his experience will lead to more success this upcoming season. Last year at North Texas, he led the team with 59 solo tackles and 113 total tackles.

At the event, coach Eric Morris repeatedly emphasized the importance of bringing in experienced players, saying he hopes that "experience pays off for us in year one."

Morris is also known for his high-flying, pass-heavy offense. Even so, Hawkins believes the scheme creates plenty of opportunities in the running game.

"Everybody knows he runs an air raid offense, and I feel some backs think that's not ideal for what they want, but I disagree," Hawkins said. "I think him spreading the ball around opens up a lot more creases for the running game."

While Hawkins toned down any major comparisons to other backs, he also admitted that his "game probably replicates Derrick Henry the most."

Hawkins credited Morris and running backs coach Patrick Cobbs for his early success at North Texas and for what he hopes to accomplish at OSU. Morris returned the praise, calling Hawkins one of the top three players he has ever coached.

Morris also spoke highly of Johnson, who has quickly earned the new coaching staff's trust.

"We've got to stop the run; that was our biggest thing last year," Johnson said. "[Defensive coordinator] Skyler Cassity constantly puts us in positions to be able to succeed, and I feel like if we have a good front seven, I know the back end is going to do what they need to do."

Johnson has been at OSU since 2022 and has experienced the program's highs and lows. He believes the defense is due for a big jump in 2026.

"[Johnson] has a great personality," Morris said. "He was probably our most disruptive player in the course of spring practice, and somebody I think that can change a football game for us."

While Johnson anchors the defense, Mestemaker has become the face of Morris' first recruiting class. Despite his excellent first year at North Texas, Mestemaker discussed improvements he can make now.

"I think last year I did struggle with that, and just kind of learning my place as a leader, because it's hard to have a leg to stand on when you haven't played at all," Mestemaker said. "But I think this year I've stepped into that role better. At the end of the day, it's honestly just earning the respect of your teammates and showing them you'll do anything."

Hawkins and Johnson immediately brought up Mestemaker when asked about the team's leaders, showing how quickly the Cowboys have rallied around their new quarterback.

Mestemaker wants to be better at his craft, whether that means building relationships, improving his understanding of the offense, or deciding when to take shots downfield. He even mentioned wanting to take a couple more deep shots this year.

Despite the roster overhaul, the Cowboys believe their expectations remain as high as ever.

"Football is football," Mestemaker said. "The Big 12, there's no one really at the top for multiple years. That lets us set our goals high… Coach Morris' whole thing is we're going to win wherever we go. Honestly, I think if you don't have that mindset, you're not in it for the right reason."
