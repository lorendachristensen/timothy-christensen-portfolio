OSU nominates Jepkirui, Stout for Big 12 Athletes of the Year

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-07-22
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State has nominated golfer Preston Stout and cross country and track standout Billah Jepkirui for the Big 12 Athletes of the Year awards.

The conference honors its top male and female athletes from the previous academic year, with winners set to be announced Thursday. Last year's winners included Colorado football’s Travis Hunter and Texas Tech softball’s Nijaree Canady.

All 16 teams submit one male and one female athlete for the conference to consider for the award.

The female athlete OSU nominated was senior Jepkirui. Originally from Kenya, Jepkirui has spent the past four years at OSU, competing in a variety of running events.

Jepkirui continued her dominant senior season by winning races in the 1,000, 1,500, 3,000 and 5,000 meters and helping OSU's 4x800 relay team post the second-fastest time in NCAA history at the Drake Relays.

Jepkirui’s nomination is tied to her performance at the Big 12 Indoor Track and Field Championships in Lubbock, Texas.

Jepkirui won the women’s mile with a 4:22.92, the fastest mile in school history and the fourth-fastest in NCAA history. The win made her the second female athlete to win three straight Big 12 mile titles. Along with a first-place finish in the 1,000-meter, she earned the Big 12 High Point Award at the event.

Stout's season followed a similar path, highlighted by another dominant performance at the Big 12 Championship.

Stout has already had a historic three-year stint in Stillwater, collecting three straight All-Big 12 selections, two straight first-team All-American selections and three straight Big 12 championships. He also contributed to the team’s 2025 national championship.

Stout’s success continued this season. He tallied victories at the Cabo Collegiate, Maridoe Intercollegiate and Mountaineer Invitational. Along with his Big 12 championship, Stout was named the Big 12 Men’s Golfer of the Year.

The highlight of Stout’s season was the individual NCAA championship he won at Omni La Costa North Course in Carlsbad, California, becoming the 10th golfer in OSU history to win a national title. After opening with a 73, Stout posted scores of 67, 65 and 69 to finish at 14-under 274, one stroke clear of Alabama's William Jennings.

The championship was Stout’s fifth win of the season, the most for any Cowboy during his junior year. The title also helped Stout become the No. 1 amateur golfer in the world.

While not all the Big 12 nominees have been announced, Stout’s resume will give him a chance to be announced as a winner on Thursday. Among those announced so far are Baylor tennis’ Devin Badenhorst and the No. 1 pick in the 2026 NBA Draft, BYU’s AJ Dybantsa.

Whether either Cowboy takes home the conference's top individual honor, both have already assembled seasons worthy of national recognition.
