Preston Stout named preseason No. 1 by PGA Tour University

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-07-22
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State’s Preston Stout has once again found himself at the top of another ranking.

Stout will begin the 2026-27 season as the top-ranked player in the PGA Tour University's Class of 2027. If he can remain in the No. 1 spot by May 31, 2027, at the end of the college season, he will earn an automatic PGA Tour membership. While there are numerous ways to gain PGA Tour membership, this would be the most straightforward and quickest way to achieve that goal.

Players who finish Nos. 2-10 earn Korn Ferry Tour membership, while those ranked 11-25 receive exempt status on PGA Tour Americas. Players would have to play up to PGA Tour membership from there.

In partnership with the World Amateur Golf Ranking, where Stout is also ranked No. 1, PGA Tour U ranks players based on the last two years of their collegiate careers. Eligible tournaments include NCAA team competitions, official PGA Tour tournaments, and select DP World Tour events.

Unsurprisingly, Stout’s last two years at OSU have been exemplary. He has earned consecutive first-team All-Big 12 and All-America selections, as well as a team national championship in 2025 and an individual national championship in 2026.

Stout has shown heavy interest in PGA competition, participating in four PGA events so far this season, making the cut for the ISCO Championship (T49) and John Deere Classic (T15) while missing the cut for the U.S. Open and the CJ Cup Byron Nelson. Stout will also be participating in the 3M Open this week.

Stout’s first three collegiate seasons have earned him this No. 1 ranking, but all this does is raise the bar for the senior. Now he will have to provide another dominant year to earn the golden ticket to PGA Tour membership.
