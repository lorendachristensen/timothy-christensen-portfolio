Bedlam basketball returns to Paycom Center in December

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-07-25
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State will once again face off against the University of Oklahoma in both men’s and women’s basketball as part of the annual Bedlam rivalry on Saturday, Dec. 12.

The doubleheader continues the tradition of the schools meeting at the Paycom Center, home of the NBA's Oklahoma City Thunder. This will mark the third consecutive year of the men’s teams playing, and the second consecutive year for the women.

Both OSU coaches will enter their matchups looking for their first career victories against OU. Steve Lutz is 0-2 against the Sooners, while Jacie Hoyt is 0-5.

The Cowboys suffered their first defeat of the season in last year’s Bedlam matchup, losing 85-76. The Sooners were led by Xzayvier Brown's 21 points and five assists. Lutz's team will look to keep Brown in check this season.

The Cowboys have made a lot of moves this offseason, bringing in On3's top-ranked transfer portal class in the Big 12, along with a consensus top 10 high school signing class. Lutz also brought on two-time national champion Gavin Roberts to his strength staff.

The Cowgirls fell to the Sooners 92-70. OSU had a rough shooting evening, making less than 38% from the field and 28% from 3-point range. Hoyt was visibly passionate on the sidelines throughout the game, receiving a technical foul for arguing a call in the second quarter. Hoyt will likely bring that same intensity this time around.

A massive overhaul marked Hoyt's offseason, as OSU lost much of last season's roster while bringing in ESPN's No. 1-ranked transfer class, including the No. 1 transfer in the country, Audi Crooks.

Fans can participate in a presale beginning at 10 a.m. Monday, July 27. Remaining tickets go on sale to the public Aug. 7, with discounted tickets available for OSU students.
