OSU basketball announces three-game exhibition slate, including Homecoming matchup

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-08-13
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State men’s basketball announced the dates of its three exhibition games in 2026, including a matchup on the Friday of “America’s Greatest Homecoming.”

New NCAA rules allow teams to play up to three preseason exhibitions against Division I opponents, an increase from two in previous years.

OSU’s first exhibition will be against New Mexico at Gallagher-Iba Arena. The game will tip off on Oct. 11. A start time has not been announced.

The exhibition will begin a home-and-home series that continues next preseason in Albuquerque.

It will be the first meeting between the two teams in Stillwater and the fourth meeting overall. New Mexico, coached by Eric Olen, made the NIT semifinals following a 26-win season in 2025.

Twelve days later, on Oct. 23, the Friday of OSU’s Homecoming celebration, the Cowboys will welcome Tulsa to GIA for an in-state matchup.

The game will tip off at 2:30 p.m. CT, serving as a lead-in to the annual Walkaround activities on the eve of the Homecoming football game.

OSU holds a 77-39 advantage in the all-time series against the Golden Hurricane. In this preseason, Tulsa will be led by fifth-year head coach Eric Konkol, following a NIT runner-up finish last season.

For its final exhibition game, OSU will travel to Madison to take on Big Ten opponent Wisconsin.

The Badgers look to be the toughest competition in the Cowboys’ exhibition lineup. Wisconsin, led by head coach Greg Gard, has made eight NCAA Tournament appearances in 11 seasons, including each of the last three years. That is the type of consistency that Steve Lutz wants to build as he enters his third year as OSU’s head coach.

After being extremely active in the transfer portal this offseason, Lutz and his team hope the exhibition games will help prepare the Cowboys for the start of the regular season.
