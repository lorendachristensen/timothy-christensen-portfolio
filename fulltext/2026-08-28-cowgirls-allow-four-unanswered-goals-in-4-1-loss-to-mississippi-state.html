Cowgirls allow four unanswered goals in 4-1 loss to Mississippi State

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-08-28
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State soccer was unable to pass its first road test of the season, falling to Mississippi State 4-1.

Due to concerns about inclement weather, the match was moved to a 5 p.m. start time. The earlier start meant the match began with a triple-digit heat index in Starkville, Mississippi.

The Cowgirls (1-2-1) struck first in the heat, scoring a goal at just the third minute of the match. With the corner-kick assist from midfielder Addy Lang, defender Brenna Rowlett headed in her first goal of the season.

MSU (4-0-0) answered at the 18th and 23rd minute marks with goals from Eden Nicholson and Hailey Johnson, respectively.

Former Cowgirl Reganne Morris got the ball on a breakaway in the 40th minute. OSU goalkeeper Logan Marks crept too far up the field, and Morris was able to sneak the ball into the bottom right corner for the Bulldogs’ third goal.

With only 34 seconds left in the half, Cowgirl Victoria Madison was called for a foul inside the penalty box after a video review. Kyli Switalski took advantage of the penalty kick, scoring on the left side of the goal.

The second half was uneventful for both teams, with neither scoring another goal. MSU finished with six shots on goal compared to OSU's one.

The loss was the second straight for the Cowgirls, and they remain winless in Power Four games since the start of last season. OSU will finish its nonconference schedule at home and will look to rebound on Sunday at noon when the Cowgirls host Grambling State.
