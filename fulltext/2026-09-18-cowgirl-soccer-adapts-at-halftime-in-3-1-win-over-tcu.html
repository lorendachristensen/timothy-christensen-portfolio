Cowgirl soccer ‘adapts’ at halftime in 3-1 win over TCU

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-09-18
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State rode a second-half wave with three goals to beat No. 20 TCU 3-1 in the Big 12 opener.

Both teams came into the matchup with physicality and toughness. The energy was palpable inside Neal Patterson Stadium despite the lack of scoring. TCU (3-3-1, 0-1-0 Big 12) controlled possession for much of the first half, but OSU head coach Colin Carmichael was willing to accept it.

“It was honestly out of respect for them,” Carmichael said. “They possess the ball really well, no matter who they play against. So, we had a choice: you either press them or you drop off. And we chose to drop off and try to frustrate them a little bit, and that's kind of what we did.”

Avery Bass' strong defense and goalkeeping kept the Horned Frogs scoreless at the half. OSU (5-2-2, 1-0-0 Big 12) was also held scoreless, as the team couldn't capitalize on its few chances near the goal. The second half was immediately a different story.

After Gracie Bindbeutel’s shot was blocked, OSU center forward Sonora DeFini corralled the rebound and scored the first goal of the night in the 46th minute. The play came in the first minute of the new half and set the tone for the rest of the game.

OSU kept the momentum going with a header from Keaton Mitchell and a right corner shot from Emmy Hedden, giving the Cowgirls a 3-0 advantage through the 67th minutes. Despite a late goal from TCU’s Emma Yolinsky in the 75th minute, the orange explosion was too much for the Horned Frogs to overcome.

After the game, Carmichael discussed what he told his team at halftime.

“We just talked about how the first half was really good,” Carmichael said. “[TCU’s] a really good team, so we talked about their adjustments and how we could adapt to that. We just said we're defending really well, but we have to be able to get up the field and cause them a little bit more of a problem. And I thought second half, we did that.”

The halftime adjustments flipped the script for the Cowgirls in multiple ways. The win was OSU’s first Big 12 win since 2024, and its first win over TCU since 2017. Whatever changes were made, Carmichael will want to emulate this success for the rest of the season.
