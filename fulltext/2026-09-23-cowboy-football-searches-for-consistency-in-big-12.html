Cowboy football searches for consistency in Big 12

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-09-23
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State football will seek its first Big 12 win since 2023 as the team travels to Morgantown, W. Va., for Saturday’s matchup against West Virginia.

Over the last two seasons, OSU is 0-18 in Big 12 play. The results were abrupt for a team that had made the Big 12 Championship the year before the drought started.

Despite the previous struggles in the conference, there is plenty of hope for OSU to compete at a high level in the Big 12 this year. With a revamped 2026 roster, head coach Eric Morris’ team has already ended the Cowboys’ FBS losing streak by beating Oregon and followed up with a 59-0 win over Murray State.

Wide receiver Miles Coleman has been a key contributor to OSU's early success, and he doesn't think the team needs to change much as they gear up for conference play.

“[The mindset] kind of stays the same,” Coleman said. “I mean, we obviously know the Big 12 is different, and these games matter a lot more than the other games. But I think just staying consistent each and every week will help us compete really well in the Big 12.”

Coleman is one of the many North Texas transfers Morris brought to Stillwater, and the group hopes to keep producing as it adjusts to Power Four competition.

Morris has had experience in the Big 12 from his time at Texas Tech, and he said before the season that he is ready to put OSU football back on the map.

“I've been in the Big 12 as a player and a coach, and Oklahoma State was so good in my memories,” Morris said. “They were a bunch of eight-, nine-, 10-win teams, and won the Big 12 in 2011, so I didn't have any of these bad memories. When I got there, I had to sit back and realize that there have been kids in this program that have been there for two years that hadn't experienced walking in that locker room after winning the conference game.”

While Morris mentioned trying to win a single conference game, some players went further. Defensive lineman Jaleel Johnson was on the OSU team that made the Big 12 championship in 2023, and he was clear in what he wanted coming into the year.

“The standard for us is a Big 12 Championship,” Johnson said at the Big 12 Media Days on July 7. “Winning just a couple games isn't gonna show. We want to put, we want our team to be on top, so obviously we struggled this past few years, these guys care… We care about each other, and I really feel like we have a great chance of bringing that trophy home.”

OSU’s Big 12 journey starts against an undefeated West Virginia team that beat No. 25 Virginia last week. The game will be OSU's first road trip outside Oklahoma and could provide an early indication of where the Cowboys stand in a deep Big 12 race.

Despite opening the season with a loss at Tulsa, the Cowboys have rebounded with wins in their last two games and are playing their best football. They will have to continue that momentum as they try to compete in the Big 12.
