OSU ranked in AP Poll for first time since 2024

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-09-27
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

After two straight winless years in the Big 12, Oklahoma State football is ranked No. 19 in the country in Sunday’s AP Top 25 poll.

It has been a long road since OSU was last ranked on Sept. 22, 2024. In that time, the Cowboys finished 3-9 and 1-11 seasons and suffered a 21-game FBS losing streak.

The No. 19 ranking reflects the transformation that OSU underwent in the offseason. North Texas head coach Eric Morris was selected to lead the next era of Cowboy football, bringing a wave of transfers to Stillwater.

Morris and his former UNT players are no strangers to national recognition. Last season, the Mean Green made their first appearance in the AP Top 25 since 1959.

The Top 25 nod is an accomplishment in an erratic season so far for OSU. The Cowboys opened the season with a disappointing loss to in-state rival Tulsa. Despite the early misstep, Morris and his team have since bounced back against No. 6 Oregon, Murray State and Big 12 rival West Virginia on the road.

Last week, WVU and OSU received the third- and fifth-most AP votes outside the Top 25, so the winner was likely to earn a spot in the rankings. OSU secured the 41-24 win with 389 passing yards from quarterback Drew Mestemaker and 192 receiving yards from pass catcher Wyatt Young.

AP rankings carry no direct effect on postseason selection, but they remain a measuring stick for national perception. For OSU, the ranking indicates how quickly the program has recovered from consecutive last-place finishes in conference play.

For a program that endured back-to-back disheartening seasons, the ranking serves as another milestone in OSU's turnaround. Just four games into Morris' tenure, the Cowboys have snapped their 19-game Big 12 losing streak and 21-game FBS losing streak.

The Cowboys still have plenty left to prove, but they now have the nation’s attention.
