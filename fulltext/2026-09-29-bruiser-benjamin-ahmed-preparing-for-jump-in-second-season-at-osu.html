‘Bruiser’: Benjamin Ahmed preparing for jump in second season at OSU

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-09-29
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Forward Benjamin Ahmed brings consistency to an offseason of change for Oklahoma State men’s basketball.

The Cowboys have made major moves this offseason, bringing in the top-ranked transfer portal class in the Big 12, according to On3, as well as a consensus top-10 high school signing class. While fans are likely excited about the new talent, head coach Steve Lutz will also see progression from players on last year’s team.

Ahmed is one of four returning players for the Cowboys. Entering his sophomore season, Ahmed said he was taking advantage of a second season in Lutz’s system.

“Just getting in better shape, and you kind of know what to expect in practice,” Ahmed said. “Just try to be a leader. Sometimes you already know how Coach Lutz is gonna come into practice and what he expects and stuff like that. You already know, so [I’m] just letting the new guys know too. This is standard right here. This is what he wants, and this is what he expects from us.”

Coming out of prep school, Ahmed was a four-star recruit, ranked as high as No. 54 nationally and No. 4 among centers by Rivals. At Putnam Science Academy, Ahmed helped his team reach three straight Prep Championship appearances, winning two.

Ahmed’s 2025-26 season at OSU did not start perfectly, with a foot injury forcing the freshman to miss the first 10 games of the campaign.

After returning, Ahmed worked his way back from injury off the bench. He showed noticeable progress during the year and started in OSU’s final six games, averaging 10 points, five rebounds and 23 minutes in that stretch.

Now that he is working toward a full offseason of training, Ahmed feels better prepared to start the season strong.

“[I’m] just quicker,” Ahmed said. "Certain ways I'm moving now, I wasn't moving [last season], like blocking shots and stuff like that. I just feel better, and I just feel great and comfortable to go out there and run. So I just feel better than the way I felt last season.”

One of Ahmed’s new teammates is Jordan Burks, and he came up with a one-word summary of Ahmed.

“Bruiser,” Burks said. “He’s just a tough guy, and he works hard. I love it. Like he just got to keep [going] day by day, just adding on 1% better every day… He's still doing everything he does. Just need to keep getting better every day.”

If Ahmed continues the progression he started in 2025, he could be a valuable piece for the Cowboys this season.
