UCF quarterback uncertainty adds another layer to OSU matchup

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-10-07
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

UCF could field multiple quarterbacks when they travel to Stillwater to face off against Oklahoma State.

The Knights didn’t enter the season expecting this much quarterback uncertainty. But injuries to the top two options have changed the team’s predicament.

Over the offseason, UCF recruited senior dual-threat quarterback Alonza Barnett III from James Madison.

Barnett spent three years at JMU and showed progress each year. The 2025 season was his breakout year, as he accounted for 3,395 yards of total offense and 38 touchdowns for the Dukes. The performance earned Barnett the Sun Belt Conference Player of the Year award and helped lead James Madison to the College Football Playoff.

The hype around Barnett's arrival at UCF mirrored the hype around Drew Mestemaker's arrival at OSU. The hope was that Barnett could carry his JMU production to the Big 12.

Plans took a detour when Barnett suffered a hamstring injury on the final drive of his second game against Pittsburgh. The injury has kept Barnett out of UCF’s last three games, and he is listed as questionable for Saturday’s game against OSU.

However, UCF head coach Scott Frost said Monday that he does not expect Barnett to play.

“I don't think it'll happen this week,” Frost said. “We got a bye week coming up after this one. We got to pour everything into this one, and hopefully that gives us time to get a lot of guys some more time to get healthy.”

If Barnett is out, UCF will likely turn to senior quarterback Keyone Jenkins to lead the offense once again. Jenkins spent three years at FIU before transferring to UCF over the offseason.

Jenkins has started in all three of Barnett’s absences, passing for 499 yards, three touchdowns and two interceptions.

This will mark the second meeting between OSU head coach Eric Morris and Jenkins. In 2023, Jenkins passed for 291 yards and a touchdown for the Panthers against a Morris-led North Texas. Morris showed respect for the senior quarterback on Monday.

“I think Jenkins is a good player, a dual-threat quarterback,” Morris said. “… I watched it firsthand. Jenkins, I think maybe my second game at UNT, he was the quarterback at [FIU] and had a phenomenal day against us. So, he's a really good football player. We have to keep him contained.”

Jenkins left his last game against Houston late in the third quarter, then returned early in the fourth. Frost said after the game that the quarterback was dealing with cramps, meaning there should be no long-term worries.

Sophomore quarterback Kaleb Annett filled in for Jenkins during his brief absence. A transfer from Boise State, Annett threw his first collegiate passes, completing 2-for-3 attempts for 6 yards. If Jenkins leaves again, the Cowboys can expect to see some Annett action at Boone Pickens Stadium.

While Jenkins is the most likely starter, OSU still must prepare for the possibility of seeing either Barnett or Annett on Saturday.
