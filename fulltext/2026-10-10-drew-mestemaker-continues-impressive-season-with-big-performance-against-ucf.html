Drew Mestemaker continues impressive season with big performance against UCF

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-10-10
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

No. 18 Oklahoma State found itself in a shootout Saturday but beat UCF 42-28 behind another standout performance from quarterback Drew Mestemaker.

Mestemaker finished his day with 304 passing yards, two touchdowns and a 68 % completion rate for the Cowboys (4-1, 2-0 Big 12). Just as he did against Oregon, Mestemaker also found success on the ground, running in a touchdown and a two-point conversion.

The performance was Mestemaker’s fourth straight game with over 300 passing yards, and a colossal 20 straight with over 200.

Head coach Eric Morris praised his quarterback’s performance.

“I thought that he had a great two weeks of preparation,” Morris said. “I thought his eyes were really good, and really didn't put the ball in harm's way the whole entire night. Just testament to him, made some unbelievable throws and put it into space, and was able to keep us for the most part ahead of the chains. I thought he managed the game really well.”

Mestemaker spent much of the matchup looking deep downfield, averaging 16 yards per completion. Out of his 19 completions on the day, six were completions over 20 yards.

The quarterback also avoided throwing an interception for the first time since Murray State, where he sat most of the second half. Turnovers were a point of emphasis for Morris and the coaching staff.

“That's something that was one of our goals in our quarterback room this week, and said it was a really big thought,” Morris said. “You know, probably 32 or maybe even 48-font on the front of it: ‘Let's not turn the ball over.’”

Mestemaker did not want the emphasis to change how he played.

“Obviously those past games and turning the ball over is not my game,” Mestemaker said. “And I'm glad to make it through one without [a turnover], but it's not like I was going into this game saying, ‘don't throw picks.’… [I] just stick to my process and go out there and get through the reads and let it rip.”

Mestemaker has continued to build an impressive resume through the early parts of this season. The quarterback has bounced back from his disappointing performance in the opener at Tulsa, with the offense scoring 39 or more points in the last four matchups.

Mestemaker’s performance Saturday is sure to continue pulling him into the national conversation. However, he is not interested in the hype.

“It's cool to hear, but at the end of the day, we got to keep taking it one week at a time,” Mestemaker said. “And you know you can't get caught up in that, because if we start losing, I'm not going to be the No. 1 pick in the [NFL] draft. So that's not my goal. It's not what I'm set on or whatever. I'm just trying to win games.”

The TU game is one of the only things holding Mestemaker back from being the front-runner in national conversations like No. 1 pick or Heisman. But if he continues this run, he might become impossible to ignore.
