Oklahoma State equestrian extends Larry Sanchez through 2031

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-10-23
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State equestrian head coach Larry Sanchez has agreed to a contract extension that keeps him in Stillwater through the 2030-31 season.

Sanchez signed the contract early in his 28th season at OSU. Sanchez is the only coach OSU equestrian has ever had, building the program since 1999.

The Cowgirls have enjoyed tremendous success under Sanchez, collecting 203 wins, including 76 over Big 12 opponents. Sanchez’s biggest accomplishment was in 2022, when the team won the National Collegiate Equestrian Association (NCEA) Championship.

Sanchez’s teams have dominated the Big 12, winning 12 conference championships, including the previous six since 2021.

Sanchez has also won two NCEA Coach of the Year and six Big 12 Coach of the Year awards. Athletic Director Chad Weiberg praised Sanchez in a press release from OSU Athletics.

“Larry Sanchez is the first and only coach we’ve had for our equestrian program, and he is a great representative of OSU Athletics and Oklahoma State University,” Weiberg said. “He has been, and continues to be, a positive influence in the lives of hundreds of our student-athletes.

“During his tenure, our program has had continued success, including winning the last six Big 12 Championships and the 2022 National Championship. We’re grateful for his continued leadership.”
