Headset Check: Conservative second quarter pays off for Cowboys

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-10-25
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Oklahoma State head coach Eric Morris showed a willingness to adapt in the Cowboys’ 57-24 homecoming win over Colorado.

Last week at Houston, OSU had notable red-zone struggles. One of the more memorable moments of the game was the offense failing to convert a fourth-and-goal opportunity from the 2-yardline.

Morris said after the game that he chased some again and that he would be doing some “soul searching”. The coach seemed to change his approach this week.

OSU found itself in two situations in the second quarter when Morris had to make tough fourth-down decisions. One was on fourth-and-4 on CU’s 29-yardline and the other was fourth-and-goal on CU’s 6-yardline.

Morris decided to kick a field goal in both circumstances. The choices showed the confidence Morris had in his team despite the defense’s rough first half.

The preserved momentum from ending the drives with field goals helped the team explode in the second half, outscoring the Buffaloes 34-0.

The decisions are easier with a trustworthy kicker, and that is something that Morris has.

Kicker Sam Keltner has been perfect this season, making all 18 field goals he has attempted. Keltner has been pleased with the kicking team’s operations this season.

“It's been awesome,” Keltner said. “I couldn't really have drawn it up any better, and I'm confident right now. I'd say a lot of that's due to my holder, snapper and the guys up front. I mean, they've been phenomenal all year. Yeah, it's been great.”

Keltner is also perfectly content to continue kicking field goals on fourth down.

“I'm sure Drew [Mestemaker] can answer that one better than me, but I just go out there and do my job,” Keltner said. “It's important to get points... We sometimes take points down there, and we sometimes don't. But yeah, I mean, just points on the board are always good, and so coming away with three instead of nothing is always coming forward.”
