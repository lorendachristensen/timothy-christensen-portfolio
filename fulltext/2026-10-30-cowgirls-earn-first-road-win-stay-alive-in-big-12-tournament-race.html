Cowgirls earn first road win, stay alive in Big 12 tournament race

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-10-30
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

The Cowgirls earn their first road win of the season, keeping postseason hopes alive.

Oklahoma State remains in the race for the final seed of the Big 12 tournament after the Cowgirls won their first road game of the season, beating Iowa State 2-0.

OSU (7-7-4 overall, 3-5-2 Big 12) was 0-4 on the road before its trip to Ames. To stay alive for the Big 12 tournament, the team needed to flip that script in the final two regular-season games.

The Cowgirls made their statement early. In the third minute, midfielder Emma Jensen got a shot right in front of the goal on an assist from Laudan Wilson. Jensen took advantage and nailed the left-footed goal to take the 1-0 lead.

A short time later, in the 15th minute, Raquelle Mitchell got the ball on a breakaway and made an extra pass to Sonora DeFini, who scored her eighth goal of the season. No ISU (4-10-4 overall, 0-7-2 Big 12) defender tracked the Cowgirls’ season-leading scorer, leaving the net wide open.

OSU handled business the rest of the way. Head coach Colin Carmichael seemed to decide to sit back to preserve the two-goal lead. Neither team scored after the 15th minute. OSU goalkeeper Avery Bass made five saves to preserve the shutout.

The win is Carmichael’s 100th career Big 12 victory. After a winless conference last season, the coach reached the milestone in his 22nd year coaching the Cowgirls.

The win also kept OSU’s chances of a Big 12 tournament berth intact. The top eight teams in the conference will compete in the tournament. OSU entered the night as the 11th seed with eight points. The Cowgirls’ win over ISU added three points, raising their total to 11.

West Virginia currently holds the eighth seed in the tournament with 13 points and two games left in its season. Along with WVU, the Cowgirls will want to watch Arizona and Arizona State, both at 10 points, as they meet in the first of their last two matches.

The only thing the Cowgirls can control is earning another three points next week in Boulder when they take on No. 17 Colorado.
