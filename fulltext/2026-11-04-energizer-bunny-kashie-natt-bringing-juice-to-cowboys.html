'Energizer Bunny' Kashie Natt bringing juice to Cowboys

By Timothy Christensen  /  The O'Colly  /  2026-11-04
This is an archived copy preserved for Timothy Christensen's portfolio. The original was published in The O'Colly at this link; this copy is shown because the original page is no longer reachable.

Kashie Natt made his Oklahoma State debut Monday when the Cowboys played Incarnate Word, and he is already having an impact.

Although Natt’s shot was not falling, he made plenty of plays for the Cowboys. Natt was all over the floor, making life difficult for the Cardinals. Natt sneaked into all sorts of passing lanes and stifled UIW before the team could set up plays.

Natt ended the night with only one block and two steals. While the fifth-year senior’s box-score numbers were modest, his influence extended beyond the stat sheet.

Several times, UIW guards were forced to pick up their dribble or abandon passing options because of Natt's pressure.

Jacob Walker appreciated his teammate despite the 2-of-9 shooting performance.

“Kash[ie] is a guy that can obviously do a lot more than just score the ball, and that's one of his superpowers,” Walker said. “And obviously once he can go out and maybe his shot is not falling. Obviously, he's a guy that gives so much effort, so he can do other things like rebound for us. He could get steals. You know what I'm saying? So just having a teammate like that… it's amazing.”

Walker and Natt both played together last season, but not in Stillwater. The duo played at Sam Houston, where the team went 22-12. Natt collected 17 blocks and 69 steals last season, helping him win Conference USA Defensive Player of the Year.

Natt and Walker have made their way to OSU, and Walker credits Natt with helping in the transition.

“He makes a lot of people's job easier,” Walker said. “So obviously having him come from Sam Houston, it makes my job easier… It makes [the team’s] job easier.”

Improving defensively was a major priority for OSU in the transfer portal. Associate head coach James Miller, who plays a significant role in recruiting, helped bring in Natt and has been impressed by what he has shown over the offseason.

“His mindset, his toughness, his care factor,” Miller said. “He's crazy, like it is really cool to watch… he's just Energizer Bunny, can't be denied. His care factor is through the roof.

“… I haven't been around anybody like him ever because he's a freak. It's really cool. So, that being said, those guys feed off that.”

Natt’s defensive emphasis contributed to OSU’s dominant first half against UIW, where it only allowed 17 points. The second half left more to be desired, letting the Cardinals score 43 points. Head coach Steve Lutz wished the team had maintained its dominance in the second half.

“I thought that our first half, we were very aggressive,” Lutz said. “We had a lot of deflections. We imposed our will on the ball. The second half obviously was a different story… I'm not taking anything away from Incarnate Word, but we lost the second half, right? And that's unacceptable.”

Time will tell whether Natt can harness his energetic style of play for a full game of action. If he can, he will continue to set the tone for the new-look Cowboys this season.
